# Lainis Kamaldiņš: Member of the 5th Saeima

**Source:** Newspaper *Lauku Avīze* (20 July 1993)

This voting record documents the activity of **Lainis Kamaldiņš** in the restored 5th Saeima (Parliament) of the Republic of Latvia, representing the "Latvijas Ceļš" (Latvian Way) faction.

## Analysis

1.  **Political Career:** Lainis Kamaldiņš was elected to the 5th Saeima (1993–1995) from the "Latvijas Ceļš" list. This marks the family's representation in the highest legislative power immediately after the restoration of independence.
2.  **Decision Making:** An "abstain" vote regarding the Agriculture Committee suggests a specific political stance or strategy within the faction.
